## The rule

Two lanes, every week, in this order:

1. **This week's material first.** Slides, then its Drill Room topic. Never
   trade it for backlog — a week you skip now becomes more backlog.
2. **Then one backlog item** from the queue below, top to bottom. No choosing.

A session is one item, 45–60 minutes. Six sessions a week is enough. On a bad
day the minimum is 25 minutes on the next item, and that counts.

## Notebooks: stop reading them

The twelve core notebooks are about 1 800 cells, but only **40 of those are
"Your turn" cells** — the rest is the lecturer's walkthrough. And the exam has
no computer. So a notebook gets **45 minutes, hard cap**:

1. Do the topic in the Drill Room first, so the notebook is confirmation.
2. Kernel → Restart & Run All. Scroll, looking at plots and outputs only.
3. Search for `Your turn` and do those cells. Nothing else.
4. Timer goes: close it, tick it, move on.
